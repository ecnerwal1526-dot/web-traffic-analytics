# web-traffic-analytics
Here's a full breakdown of what the dashboard reveals:

**Traffic growth is healthy, but session quality is slipping.** Page views are up 12.4% and unique sessions grew 8.1%, yet average session duration dipped slightly — users are arriving more but exploring a little less deeply per visit. The improved bounce rate (down 2.1%) is a bright spot.

**Organic search dominates at 44%**, which signals strong SEO health. Paid traffic at 16% is the smallest channel — worth evaluating cost-per-acquisition against the conversion funnel below.

**The funnel tells the real story.** The steepest drop-off happens between the product/detail page (42.6K) and add-to-cart (21.3K) — a 50% drop. This is the highest-priority friction point. The checkout-to-conversion step also loses 48%, which is typical but improvable with trust signals, progress indicators, and simplified form flows.

**Desktop users are far more engaged** at nearly 5 minutes average session duration vs. 2.5 minutes on mobile — a 2× gap that suggests the mobile experience likely needs layout, load speed, or navigation improvements.

**`/pricing` and `/checkout` have the highest exit rates** (41% and 47%). These are decision-critical pages where users should be converting, not leaving — common fixes include adding social proof, clarifying value propositions, and reducing form complexity.
